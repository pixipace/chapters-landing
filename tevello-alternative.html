Chapters vs Tevello

A Tevello alternative that's free until you sell.

Chapters and Tevello both sell courses through your store's native Shopify checkout. The real difference is when you pay: Tevello is a flat $29 a month from day one, while Chapters has no monthly fee — we earn 5% only when a course actually sells, capped at $29 a month.

The short version

Tevello is a well-reviewed, all-inclusive course and community app at a flat $29/month, and if you want membership communities or you're already selling steadily, that flat fee is simple and predictable. Chapters takes the opposite approach: install free, pay nothing until a course, exam, or certificate actually sells, then a 5% commission that's capped at $29 a month — so you never pay more than Tevello's flat fee, and often less. If you're launching your first course, still building your library, or your sales fluctuate month to month, the free-until-you-sell model keeps your costs at zero while you get there. Both apps sell through native Shopify checkout, so either way your students buy on your own store.

Why the model matters

A subscription bills while you build. A commission waits until you sell.

Flat pricing isn't a flaw — it's simple and predictable, and Tevello is upfront that all features are included in one subscription. The question is timing. Most course businesses spend their first months recording, editing, and building an audience before revenue arrives, and a subscription bills through all of it. A commission doesn't.

  • While you build: a $0-sales month costs $0 on Chapters and $29 on a flat subscription. Three months of building before launch is $0 vs $87.
  • The break-even point is $580/month in course sales — that's where 5% equals $29. Below it, Chapters costs less than a $29 subscription. At or above it, the cap kicks in and both cost the same $29.
  • The cap means no worst case: whether you sell $600 or $20,000 in a month, the Chapters commission stops at $29 — the same number as Tevello's flat fee, never more.

To be fair

What Tevello does well — and when it might fit you better.

Tevello is a genuinely well-regarded app: as of August 2026 its listing shows a 5.0 rating across 671 reviews, it carries the Built for Shopify badge, and its single $29 plan includes unlimited courses, members, and communities along with memberships, subscriptions, drip content, quizzes, certificates, and bundles. It also ships larger default video allowances than we do — 100 GB of storage and 500 GB of monthly bandwidth included — and lets you embed YouTube, Vimeo, or Wistia videos without counting against those limits.

Tevello may be the better fit if community is the heart of your offer — its membership communities, challenges, and subscription tools go further than what Chapters does today — or if you simply prefer one fixed, predictable bill and you're already selling comfortably past the point where the two apps cost the same. If instead you want exams, verifiable certificates, and a cost that stays at zero until revenue shows up, that's the corner we've built Chapters for.

FAQ

Tevello vs Chapters, answered.

Is there a free alternative to Tevello for Shopify?+

Chapters is free to install with no monthly fee and no plans or tiers. You pay a 5% commission only when a course, exam, retake, or paid certificate actually sells — capped at $29 a month — and in a month with no sales you pay nothing. Video hosting is included, with 30 GB of storage and 300 GB of monthly student streaming free. See the full pricing breakdown.

What does Tevello cost?+

As of August 2026, per Tevello's public listing and pricing page, Tevello is a single paid plan at $29 per month with a 14-day free trial (plus a free Development plan for development stores). Their pricing page states they don't charge fees per course or on your sales, and their default video allowance is 100 GB of storage with 500 GB of monthly bandwidth. Always confirm current pricing on their official pages.

What's the main difference between Chapters and Tevello?+

The pricing model. Both apps sell courses through native Shopify checkout, but Tevello is a flat $29/month subscription from day one, while Chapters has no subscription at all — we take 5% only when you sell, capped at $29/month. The break-even is $580/month in course sales: below that, Chapters costs less; above it, both cost the same $29. Feature-wise, Chapters leans into exams, verifiable certificates, and secure video; Tevello leans into communities, memberships, and subscriptions.

Does Chapters use native Shopify checkout like Tevello?+

Yes. Chapters courses sell as native Shopify products at your own store's checkout — with your payment methods, your multi-currency settings, and instant enrollment the moment payment lands, including guest checkout. Students then learn in a branded portal on your own domain that they can install to their phone's home screen.

How do I switch from Tevello to Chapters?+

There's no automated migration between course apps, so switching means rebuilding: install Chapters free, upload your videos (hosting is included — no separate video host needed), recreate your chapters, lessons, quizzes, and certificates, and publish — each course becomes a native Shopify product. Because Chapters has no monthly fee, you can build and test everything alongside your current setup and only move students over when you're ready. Our setup guide walks through it, and chapters@pixipace.com reaches a human if you get stuck.
