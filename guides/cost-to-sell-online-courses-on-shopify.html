How much does it cost to sell online courses on Shopify?

By Gill · builder of Chapters LMS · Updated August 2026

If you already run a Shopify store, selling online courses adds exactly one new cost: the course app. Apps range from free-until-you-sell — Chapters is $0 up front, then a 5% commission capped at $29/month — to flat subscriptions between $19.99 and $199.99/month (competitor figures here and below are from their public pricing pages as of August 2026). Your Shopify plan and your payment processing stay exactly what you pay today, because courses sell at your store's normal checkout like any other product.

What does it actually cost to sell courses on Shopify?

Your total is a stack of three things. Two of them you're probably paying already.

1. Your Shopify plan. Courses don't need a special plan or an upgrade. They sell as normal digital products through the checkout you already have, so whatever your store costs today, it costs the same with courses added. Chapters works on every Shopify plan.

2. Payment processing. Every course sale goes through your store's regular checkout, so it carries the same processing rate as any other order — the same slice you already give up on a t-shirt or a candle. Course apps don't change that rate, and it applies equally no matter which app you pick.

3. The course app. This is the only genuinely new line item, and it's where prices vary wildly — from $0 up front to nearly $200 a month. The rest of this guide is about getting this one right.

One thing to check before comparing app prices: whether video hosting is included. If it isn't, a separate host like Vimeo or Wistia becomes a fourth line item. Chapters hosts your video on a global CDN as part of the app, so there's nothing extra to subscribe to — you upload straight into the course builder.

How much do Shopify course apps cost in 2026?

Course app pricing comes in four shapes. Competitor numbers below are as of August 2026, per their public pricing pages — always confirm on the official sites.

Flat subscription: Tevello at $29/month

Tevello charges a single $29/month plan with a 14-day trial and no per-sale fees. It includes 100 GB of video storage and 500 GB of monthly bandwidth; pricing for going past those isn't published.

Flat pricing is simple and predictable. The catch is timing: it bills the same $29 in the months you're still recording lessons as it does in your best sales month.

Tiered by lesson count: Courses Plus at $19.99–$199.99/month

Courses Plus prices by the size of your curriculum: a free Demo plan capped at 5 active lessons, then $19.99/month for 20 lessons, $79.99/month for 100, and $199.99/month for unlimited. No transaction fees on any tier.

If your course is small and stays small, the $19.99 tier is one of the cheaper subscriptions around. The catch is that lesson counts tend to grow, and each threshold you cross roughly quadruples the monthly bill.

Hosted platform plus fees: Teachable at $39+/month

Teachable isn't a Shopify app — it's a separate platform with its own site, checkout, and customer accounts. Its Starter plan is $39/month (or $29 on annual billing) plus a 7.5% transaction fee on every sale. Builder at $89/month and Growth at $189/month drop the fee to 0%.

For a Shopify merchant, the real cost isn't just the price — it's running a second storefront. Your students check out somewhere else, with a different account, and your orders live in two systems.

Commission-capped: Chapters at $0 until you sell

Chapters has no subscription at all. It's free to install with every feature included — unlimited courses, unlimited students, quizzes, exams, certificates, drip, and video hosting. When a course or exam sells, Chapters takes 5% of that sale, and the commission is capped at $29/month total.

The math has a useful pivot point: 5% of $580 is $29. Below $580/month in course sales, you pay less than any flat $29 subscription. Above it, the cap kicks in and you pay $29 — never more, no matter how much you sell. In a month with zero sales, you pay zero.

The allowances: 30 GB of video storage free, then $0.16/GB/month billed automatically (you're emailed before an overage ever bills), and 300 GB of monthly student streaming free, then $0.08/GB. Everything lands on your normal Shopify invoice — no separate card.

Hidden costs to check before you pick an app

Video hosting limits. Every app that hosts video has an allowance, and they're not comparable at a glance. Tevello includes more up front (100 GB storage, 500 GB bandwidth) but doesn't publish overage pricing; Chapters includes less free (30 GB, 300 GB) but publishes exact overage rates, so you can do the math before you hit the line.

Tier cliffs. Per-lesson tiers feel cheap until your curriculum grows. Adding lesson 21 on Courses Plus moves you from $19.99 to $79.99/month.

Annual lock-in. The cheapest advertised prices on hosted platforms usually assume annual billing — Teachable's "$29" is really $348 paid up front. Monthly billing costs more.

Feature paywalls. Some pricing pages put quizzes, certificates, or drip content on higher tiers. Chapters ships everything on the one free-to-install plan — the full feature list is the same for every store.

Which pricing model fits your store?

Full disclosure: I built Chapters, so read these recommendations knowing that. The numbers, though, are just arithmetic.

Selling steadily above $580/month, every month? A flat $29 subscription and Chapters' capped commission cost you the same $29.

At that point price stops being the deciding factor, so pick on features instead. If exams, retakes, and verifiable certificates matter to you, that's the corner Chapters is built for. Compare the feature lists and choose on fit, not on cost.

Small, fixed curriculum with reliable sales? Courses Plus at $19.99/month is the cheapest subscription here — as long as you genuinely stay under 20 lessons.

Starting out, pre-launch, or seasonal? Commission-capped wins, and it isn't close. You'll spend weeks or months recording before the first sale, and a subscription bills through all of it. With Chapters those months cost $0, launch month costs 5% of whatever you actually sold, and your worst-case month is only ever $29.

No Shopify store at all? Then a hosted platform like Teachable is a fair option — but if you have a store, adding a second checkout and a second customer database to save nothing on price is a hard sell.
