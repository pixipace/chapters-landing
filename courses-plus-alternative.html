The short version

Courses Plus is an established Shopify course app with a flat monthly subscription — tiers run from a free 5-lesson demo plan to $199.99/month, gated by how many active lessons you have, with no transaction fees stated on its public pages. Chapters flips the model: there's no subscription at all, every feature is included from day one, and we take 5% of each sale, capped at $29 a month. If you want memberships, subscriptions, or SCORM import, Courses Plus covers ground we don't. If you want your cost to be zero until students are actually paying you — and to never exceed $29 a month in commission after that — that's exactly what Chapters is built for.

Why the model matters

Subscriptions bill your curriculum. Commission bills your revenue.

With a lesson-gated subscription, your cost is set by how much you've built — it's the same in a month with zero sales as in your best month ever. With Chapters, cost tracks revenue instead: $0 while you build and launch, 5% of what you actually sell after, and never more than $29 a month in commission no matter how big the month gets.

  • While you build: add as many lessons, quizzes, and exams as you like — there's no lesson count pushing you into a higher tier.
  • In a slow month: no sales means no commission — the bill is $0, not a fixed fee.
  • In a big month: the cap kicks in at $29 — at $2,000 in sales that's an effective 1.45%, and it only gets smaller as you grow.

To be fair

What Courses Plus does well — and when it might fit you better.

Courses Plus has been on the Shopify App Store since January 2020, carries the Built for Shopify badge, and holds a 4.9-star rating across 206 reviews as of August 2026 — a genuinely strong track record. It also covers things Chapters doesn't: memberships and subscriptions for recurring revenue, SCORM import, free migration from Teachable, Kajabi, and Thinkific, AI-drafted course creation, and Google Meet alongside Zoom for live lessons. If recurring membership billing or a SCORM library is central to your business — or your whole curriculum fits within 20 lessons, where their $19.99/month Basic plan can undercut our $29 monthly cap once you're selling steadily — Courses Plus may be the better fit. FAQ

Courses Plus alternative questions, answered.

Is Chapters a free alternative to Courses Plus?+

Chapters is free to install with no monthly fee and no plans or tiers — every feature is included from day one, with unlimited courses, lessons, and students. We earn a flat 5% commission only when a course or exam sells, capped at $29 a month. Video hosting is included too, with 30 GB of storage and 300 GB of monthly student streaming free. The full details are on our pricing page.

What's the main difference between Chapters and Courses Plus?+

The pricing model. As of August 2026, per its public listing, Courses Plus charges a flat monthly subscription in tiers gated by how many active lessons you have, with no transaction fees stated. Chapters has no subscription at all: it's free to install and takes 5% of each sale, capped at $29 a month — so your cost tracks your revenue instead of the size of your curriculum.

Courses Plus has no transaction fees — why does Chapters charge 5%?+

The 5% commission is how Chapters can be free until you sell. There's no fixed fee while you build and launch, nothing to pay in a month with no sales, and the commission is capped at $29 a month no matter how much you sell — at $2,000 a month in course sales that's an effective 1.45%, shrinking as you grow. It's the same trade in both directions: a flat subscription is predictable but always due; commission is $0 when your revenue is.

Does Chapters limit how many lessons or students I can have?+

No. Chapters has no caps on courses, lessons, or students — there's a single plan and it includes everything, so nothing about the size of your curriculum changes what you pay. The only metered usage is video: 30 GB of storage free (then $0.16/GB/month) and 300 GB of student streaming free each month (then $0.08/GB), measured and billed automatically, with an email warning before storage overage ever bills.

How do I switch from Courses Plus to Chapters?+

Install Chapters free alongside your current setup and rebuild your curriculum in the course builder — chapters, lessons, quizzes, and exams — then upload your videos; hosting is included, so there's nothing external to configure. Publishing a course creates a native Shopify product at your own checkout, so your store's buying experience doesn't change. Since installing is free, you can build everything and compare before switching anything off. If you'd like a hand planning the move, email chapters@pixipace.com.
