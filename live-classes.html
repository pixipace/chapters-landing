Authorize Chapters to use your existing Zoom account a single time. From then on, every live lesson you schedule creates its own Zoom meeting automatically — no per-meeting links to copy and paste, and no separate Zoom app for you to build.

  • Your account, free or paid. Use the Zoom plan you already have — meetings run on your own account.

  • Nothing to build. There's no separate Zoom integration for you to develop or maintain.

Schedule in the lesson

A live class is just another lesson.

Add a Live lesson to any chapter, set the date, time, and duration, and Chapters creates the Zoom meeting on your account. Times are handled in your store's timezone, so there's no mental math. Edit or cancel the lesson and the change syncs straight back to Zoom.

  • Set date, time, and duration right where you build the course.
  • The Zoom meeting is created automatically on your account.
  • Edit or cancel and the update syncs back to Zoom.

Students join from the course

One tap into the live room.

On the lesson page, students see a "Join live class" button with a countdown ticking down to the start. When it's time, one tap takes them straight into Zoom — no meeting IDs to find, no links to dig out of an email.

In one tap the class opens in Zoom (app or browser) — no meeting IDs or links for students to hunt down.

  • A countdown shows exactly when the class begins.
  • One tap on the lesson page opens the session in Zoom.
  • It lives alongside their other lessons, inside the course they bought.

After the session ends, attach the recording to the same lesson. It plays right there in the course, so students who couldn't make the live time can still catch every minute — and those who attended can review it.
