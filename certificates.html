The certificate

An engraved diploma, in your colors.

Each certificate is a landscape, A4-ratio PDF designed to look the part — clean engraved type, a ruled border, and the details that matter. It carries your brand from the first glance.

  • Your logo, set as the masthead

    Upload your store logo and it sits at the top of every certificate you issue.

  • Your brand color as the ink

    Set one accent color and it threads through the border, rules, and seal — so the diploma reads as yours, not a template.

  • Your signature

    Add a signature image, or type a name and have it set in an elegant script. It signs every diploma for you.

  • A wax-seal monogram & an optional score

    A seal carries your store's initials. When the credential includes graded work, the overall score is printed too.

Public verification

A credential anyone can check.

Every certificate carries a unique number and a public verification page. An employer, an admissions office, or anyone holding the PDF can open it and confirm the credential is real — no account, no login.

  • A unique certificate number on every diploma you issue.
  • The page shows the essentials — student name, credential, score, and date issued.
  • No sign-in required to confirm a certificate is genuine.

Good to know

A few honest details.

So you know exactly what you're getting before you switch certificates on.

Names print in English

Certificate names are rendered in Latin characters, so they read cleanly in the engraved type.

The artwork is fixed

The engraved layout stays the same. What changes is yours: logo, brand color, signature, seal initials, and the text.

Score shows when graded

The score line appears only when there's graded work — like a final exam. Completion-only certificates simply omit it.
