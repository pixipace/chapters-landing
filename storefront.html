Instant auto-enrollment

Paid means in.

The moment an order is paid, the buyer is enrolled and can start learning. No manual fulfillment, no "your access is on the way."

Access on payment

Enrollment fires the instant the order is paid — not when someone gets around to it. The buyer can open lesson one before the confirmation page has closed.

A welcome email, automatically

Every new student gets a welcome email the moment they're enrolled, pointing them straight at their course — no template to build first.

Guest checkout just works

No account at checkout? Buyers get emailed instructions right away, and the purchase links to their account automatically the first time they sign in.

Orders auto-fulfill

Course orders mark themselves Complete instead of sitting in an unfulfilled, "awaiting shipping" state — your order list stays clean and accurate.

Refunds soft-revoke

Refund an order and access is removed automatically — but the student's progress is preserved, so a re-purchase picks up right where they left off.

Seat caps & access windows

Cap seats and the storefront shows a live "spots left" count, then Sold out — expired seats free up again. Access is lifetime by default, or a set number of days; a re-purchase restarts the clock.

Checkout & order extensions

A thank-you that sends them to class.

Selling something digital shouldn't end on a page that talks about shipping. Chapters adds course-aware extensions to the post-purchase moments where buyers are most ready to start.

  • A course-specific thank-you experience confirms enrollment and points buyers straight at their first lesson.
  • An order-page CTA takes buyers right to their course instead of leaving them on shipping language.

One-time setup. The thank-you experience is switched on once in Shopify's checkout editor — a couple of clicks. The order-page card needs no setup at all: it appears on course orders automatically.

My Learning

Their Shopify account becomes a student dashboard.

Chapters adds a native My Learning page inside the Shopify customer account — the same place as their orders and profile. No separate login, no external site: their store account is their school account.

  • Continue where they left off — a hero card with the current course, live progress, and a one-tap resume.
  • To-dos and live classes — pending quizzes and assignments with scores, plus upcoming sessions, right on the dashboard.
  • Make the menu read like a school — put My Learning first and rename "Orders" to "Purchases" in Shopify's menu editor. The setup guide shows both.

More ways to sell

One checkout, many ways to charge.

Courses are the start. Bundles, exams, and certificates all sell through the same Shopify checkout — each one a real product on your store.

Bundles

One Shopify product can unlock multiple courses at once — sell a full curriculum at a single price, and every course in it opens on payment.

Sellable exams

Put a price on an exam and it becomes its own Shopify product. Each purchase grants a bundle of attempts — and students can buy another when they run out. See examinations.

Paid certificates

Charge for the certificate itself. Chapters creates a hidden Shopify product behind it and shows a buy button on the completion page. See certificates.

Free courses as lead magnets

Price a course at $0 to grow your audience. Free courses show a small "Powered by Chapters LMS" line under the player — any paid course removes it.

Sale pricing & badges

Set a compare-at price and the storefront shows an automatic Save % badge. A featured-course badge puts your flagship front and center in the catalog.

Free previews that convert

Mark lessons as free previews and add a course preview video with a custom poster — buyers can try before they buy, right on the product page.
