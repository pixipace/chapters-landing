Lesson comments

A place to ask, right under the lesson.

Students leave comments on the exact lesson they're stuck on, so the context never gets lost. Every comment is moderated by you — nothing appears under your lessons without your say-so.

  • Comments attached to each lesson — feedback stays next to the content it's about.
  • Moderated by you — approve, hide, or delete any comment from your admin.
  • Part of the portal — comments sit alongside bookmarks and private notes in the student experience.

Comments live in the student portal on your own domain — moderation happens in your Chapters admin.

Reviews & ratings

Proof that sells the next enrollment.

Only enrolled students can rate a course and leave a few words about it. The average rating shows with the course, so every student who finishes and loves it helps sell the next seat.

  • Reviews from enrolled students — only people who bought and joined can rate.
  • An average rating per course, always current as new reviews come in.
  • Stars plus words — a rating and a short written review from each student.

Lifecycle emails

The right email, at the right moment — automatically.

Chapters sends the emails a school needs without you lifting a finger — from the first welcome to the reminder that brings a quiet student back.

Welcome

Inactive-student reminders

Automated reminders bring back students who've gone quiet — each one with an unsubscribe link built in.

One-click nudges

The Insights dashboard flags students who need attention — one click sends a personal email nudge from you.

Automated, never spammy

Inactive-student reminders carry an unsubscribe link, and personal nudges only go out when you click send. Students get help, not noise.

Welcome emails go out the moment payment lands, grading emails when you post feedback, and inactive reminders on their own schedule — nothing for you to trigger.

Push & PWA reminders

A nudge before the live class starts.

Your portal installs to a student's phone home screen — one tap on Android, a guided install on iPhone — with your store's logo as the icon. Installed students get push notifications, including a reminder 30 minutes before every live class and another at 5 minutes.

  • Live-class push reminders at 30 minutes and again at 5 minutes before start.
  • Installable web app — your store's logo on their home screen, no app store involved.
  • Calendar downloads — every scheduled class comes with an .ics file for their calendar.

Students who need attention

Catch a student before they quietly quit.

Most students don't ask for help — they just stop showing up. The Insights dashboard keeps a triage list of enrolled students who've gone quiet, so you can bring them back while the course is still fresh.

  • A ready-made list of students who enrolled, started, then stalled.
  • One-click email nudges — reach out without leaving the dashboard.
  • Automated inactive reminders cover the rest — unsubscribe link included.
