FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does Chapters cost? +

Nothing to install. Nothing per month. We take 5% of each course sale — that's the entire pricing model. If you don't sell, you don't pay.

Do I need a special Shopify plan? +

No. Chapters works on every Shopify plan, from Basic on up.

Can students watch on phones? +

Yes. Lessons stream smoothly on phones, tablets, laptops, and TVs. Push notifications keep students coming back, even when the tab is closed.

How are my videos protected? +

Every video stream is signed and tied to the enrolled student. No public URLs, no shareable links, no easy leaks.

Can I migrate courses from another platform? +

Yes. Upload your existing videos and rebuild your structure in minutes. For larger migrations, get in touch and we'll help.

Do live classes use my own Zoom account? +

Yes. Connect your existing Zoom account (free or paid). Schedule sessions inside Chapters and students join right from their course page.

What happens if I uninstall? +

Your course data is exportable for 30 days after uninstall. You keep everything you built; you can re-install anytime.
