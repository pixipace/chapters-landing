The short version

Two good tools. One question: where does your business live?

Teachable is a complete standalone platform — a hosted "school" website with its own site builder, checkout, and payouts — and if you don't run an online store and never want to, that all-in-one approach can serve you well. But if you're a Shopify merchant, choosing Teachable means running two platforms: two checkouts, two customer lists, two sets of analytics, and a subscription that bills even in months you sell nothing. Chapters takes the other path — it's a Shopify app, so your courses sell as products at the checkout you already own, your students live in your Shopify customer base, and you pay nothing until a course actually sells. If consolidation is the goal, that's the difference that matters.

Why the model matters

A subscription bills you for months. A commission bills you for sales.

On Teachable's Starter plan at monthly billing, a slow year still costs $39 × 12 = $468 before you've sold a single course — plus 7.5% of everything you do sell. Chapters flips that: $0 until a course sells, then a flat 5%, and the total commission is capped at $29 a month no matter how much you sell. Your worst month costs nothing, and your best month never costs more than $29.

  • Nothing at $0 sales — building, uploading, and testing cost you nothing while you get the course right.
  • The cap works in your favor — past $580 in monthly course sales, the effective rate only falls. At $2,000 it's 1.45%.
  • One invoice, one platform — the commission bills through Shopify App charges on the invoice you already pay, next to the store your courses now live in.

To be fair

What Teachable does well — and when it might fit you better.

Teachable has built a genuinely complete standalone business-in-a-box: a hosted school site with its own builder, coaching and digital-download products alongside courses, up to 1 TB of video storage on its listed plans, automated remittance of U.S. sales tax and EU/UK VAT on its checkout, and BackOffice payouts that handle paying co-authors and affiliates for you. If you don't run a Shopify store and don't want to — or if automated multi-jurisdiction tax remittance and built-in author/affiliate payouts are central to how your business operates — Teachable's all-in-one model may genuinely be the better fit, and its paid plans come with a 7-day free trial and a 30-day guarantee so you can judge for yourself. Chapters makes the opposite bet: that if Shopify is already your home, your courses should live there too.

FAQ

Switching questions, answered.

Can I sell online courses on Shopify instead of Teachable?+

Yes. With Chapters, each course becomes a native Shopify product sold at your own checkout. Students are enrolled instantly when payment completes — including guest checkout — and they learn in a branded portal on your own domain at /apps/courses. Video hosting and streaming are included, so you don't need Teachable, Vimeo, or Wistia alongside your store.

How much does Chapters cost compared to Teachable?+

Chapters is free to install with no monthly fee — we earn a 5% commission on course and exam sales, capped at $29 a month, billed on your normal Shopify invoice. As of August 2026, Teachable's public pricing starts at $39/month ($29/month billed annually) on Starter, which also carries a 7.5% transaction fee; Builder and Growth are $89 and $189 per month with 0% transaction fee. In a month where you sell nothing, Chapters costs $0.

How do I move my courses from Teachable to Shopify?+

There's no one-click importer — you rebuild in Chapters, and most of it is faster than it sounds. Upload your lesson videos once to the shared media library, recreate your chapters and lessons, and bulk-import quiz and exam question banks from an Excel or CSV spreadsheet. Publishing a course creates its Shopify product automatically, so from then on students buy at your store's checkout and appear in your Shopify customer list.

Do my students need a separate website or login?+

No. On Teachable, students hold accounts on a hosted school site that lives on a teachable.com subdomain or a custom domain pointed at Teachable. With Chapters, students learn on your own domain at /apps/courses, can install the portal to their phone's home screen, and exist as customers in your own Shopify store — the same customer list as everyone else who buys from you.

Does Chapters host my course videos like Teachable does?+

Yes. Video hosting and streaming on a global CDN are included — there's no separate video host to wire up or pay for. Every store gets 30 GB of storage free ($0.16 per GB per month after, billed automatically with a warning email first) and 300 GB of student streaming free each month ($0.08 per GB after). Playback is protected with signed expiring URLs, a per-student moving watermark, and copy/print blocking.
